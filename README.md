# Egor Kletskov

AI Automation Engineer and Software Engineer building practical AI systems for business operations.

I focus on AI products that help teams move faster: workflow automation, internal copilots, retrieval systems, evaluation pipelines, model routing, and operational tooling. Most of my work sits at the intersection of backend engineering and applied AI, with an emphasis on systems that can be shipped, measured, and iterated on.

- LLM applications with structured outputs, evaluation, and guardrails
- RAG systems for policy, knowledge, and documentation-heavy environments
- Routing and gateway layers for multi-provider AI stacks
- Lightweight ML tooling for anomaly detection and business monitoring

- Treat evaluation and routing as first-class production concerns
